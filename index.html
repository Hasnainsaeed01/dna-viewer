Helix Facts — B-DNA

  • Rise: 3.4 Å per base pair
  • Base pairs per turn: 10.5
  • Pitch: 34 Å per full turn
  • Diameter: 2.0 nm
  • Handedness: right-handed double helix
  • Strands: antiparallel (5′→3′ against 3′→5′)
  • Major groove: 2.2 nm wide
  • Minor groove: 1.2 nm wide

Pairing Rules

  • A – T: 2 hydrogen bonds (weaker)
  • G – C: 3 hydrogen bonds (stronger)
  • Strand 1 runs 5′ → 3′; Strand 2 runs 3′ → 5′
  • Each strand is the exact complement of the other
